#terminology #college_studies

###### 𝑨 - Intelligible
clear, distinct, and easy enough to understand, whether spoken or written

###### 𝑨 - Scheme
an organized plan, a structured system or program, or a secret and sometimes dishonest plot to deceive others

###### 𝑨 - Ciphertext
the scrambled, unreadable output that is produced when readable information—known as **plaintext**—is converted using a mathematical algorithm and an encryption key

###### 𝑨 - Burstiness
the degree of variation in sentence length and structural complexity throughout a written text, or the intermittent, uneven clustering of events over time in systems and data

###### 𝑨 - Network jitter
the statistical variance in the arrival time of data packets over a network

###### 𝑨 - Stochastic
**random**, **probabilistic**, or involving a **random variable** that causes outcomes to vary by chance

###### 𝑨 - Attack model
a structured representation that defines an adversary's assumed capabilities, entry points, motivations, constraints, and step-by-step paths to compromise a system

###### 𝑨 - Obscurity
the state of being unknown to many people or the quality of being unclear and hard to understand
