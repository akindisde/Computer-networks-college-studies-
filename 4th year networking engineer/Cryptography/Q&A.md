###### ❔ - What are the key-management problems in cryptography?
Key management problems revolve around the secure handling of cryptographic keys throughout their entire lifecycle, encompassing secure generation, distribution, storage, rotation, and destruction. Because compromising a single key undermines the security of the entire encryption system, major challenges include securely transmitting keys over untrusted channels, protecting stored keys from unauthorized extraction or insider threats, enforcing strict access controls, and managing timely key revocation and expiration without disrupting active communications.
