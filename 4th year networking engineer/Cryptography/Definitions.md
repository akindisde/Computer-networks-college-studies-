###### 🧠 - Number theory
Number theory is a branch of pure mathematics dedicated to the study of the properties, relationships, and behavior of integers, prime numbers, and arithmetic functions. Often historically described as the queen of mathematics due to its abstract beauty, it explores foundational concepts such as divisibility, modular arithmetic, and Diophantine equations. While long valued purely for its intellectual elegance, modern number theory forms the core mathematical foundation of contemporary cryptography, securing global digital communications through complex algorithms like RSA and elliptic-curve cryptography.

###### 🧠 - Information theory
Information theory is a mathematical framework founded by Claude Shannon that deals with the quantification, storage, and transmission of data. It introduces core concepts such as entropy—which measures the uncertainty or information content of a message—alongside channel capacity and data compression limits to determine how efficiently and reliably signals can be sent across noisy mediums. By establishing the fundamental boundaries of communication, information theory underpins modern telecommunications, data storage, error-correcting codes, and computer science as a whole.

###### 🧠 - Propagation delay
the time it takes for the first bit of a signal to travel from a sender to a receiver across a communication medium, or for an output to respond to an input change in an electronic circuit

###### 🧠 - Network service rate
the speed of data transmission through a telecommunications system, a utility transmission charge, or an operational queue-processing capacity
